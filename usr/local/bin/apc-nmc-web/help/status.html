Battery and Device Info

  • Battery Install/Replace Date: read from the UPS. After a physical battery swap, press Battery Replaced so the age and the RB alarm reset correctly.
  • Model / Serial / Firmware: read from device.model, ups.serial and ups.firmware.
  • Manufacture Date: shown when the UPS reports it.

Transfers

Counts and timestamps of switches to battery/bypass. A sudden rise in transfers usually means unstable mains power or a weakening battery.

Beeper

Shows the UPS beeper state with Enable/Disable/Mute buttons (requires a permitted role). Muting silences the audible alarm; events are still logged.

When the UPS Is Offline

If the link to upsd drops, the page shows the last valid reading marked as stale and keeps retrying. See Troubleshooting.
