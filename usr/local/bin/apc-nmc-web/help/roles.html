The card has four access tiers, summarised below. Your role is fixed at login and shown beside your name in the top bar.

CapabilityAdminDeviceNetworkRead-Only

User management✔———
Backup/restore, updates, API token✔———
Clear event log / support bundle✔———

Notes

  • Device Only is for operators who manage the UPS but must not touch network settings.
  • Network-Only is the reverse — manages network/services but can't send UPS commands.
  • Read-Only is for monitoring — every page is visible but controls are hidden or disabled; forms render greyed out.
  • Controls your role can't use are either not rendered or disabled; even a hand-typed POST URL is rejected with 403.
  • RADIUS users get the role set in "Assigned Role" under RADIUS settings.
