The Home page is a live summary of the whole system — designed to be your first glance.

Tiles

TileMeaning
Battery ChargeRemaining capacity with a progress bar — orange below 50%, red below 20%
RuntimeEstimated minutes the UPS can run on battery at the current load
LoadPercent of the UPS rated capacity being drawn
OutputReal power (W) and apparent power (VA)
InputUtility mains voltage and frequency
Output VVoltage and frequency the UPS feeds to the load
Battery TempInternal UPS temperature, when the UPS reports it

Hovering a tile shows a short explanation. If the UPS is unreachable, tiles keep the last known values dimmed.

Status Pill and On-Battery Banner

  • Alarm pill (top bar): green "Normal", orange/red when alarms are active. Click it to open UPS Status.
  • On-battery banner: during an outage a banner on every page shows charge and minutes remaining.

Cards Below the Tiles

  • State: current condition, colour-coded (green ok, orange warning, red critical).
  • Alarms: active alarm conditions reported by the UPS.
  • Last transfer: when the UPS last switched to battery/bypass.
  • Recent events: the six newest log entries with severity.

Live Refresh

Content updates every 5 seconds from /status.json. The green dot by the clock means data is fresh; if it goes out, the UPS connection is down.
