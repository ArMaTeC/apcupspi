Event Log

Logs › Event Log lists every event: power-state changes, alarms, logins, commands sent, errors and service events.

  • Severity: colour-coded — grey Info, orange Warning, red Critical. Use the severity checkboxes to hide levels.
  • Filter: the text box filters rows instantly (client-side).
  • Clear Log: (admin) wipes the history — not reversible.
  • Download: events.txt serves the raw log for archiving or parsing.

Data Log

Logs › Data Log samples UPS readings (voltage, load, charge, temperature, …) on a timer and charts them.

  • Chart: the 24 h / 7 days / All buttons change the displayed window (default 24 h).
  • Table: rows lazy-load as you scroll (150 at a time) so the page stays fast; the footnote shows "x of y samples shown".
  • Export: data.txt is the raw log; ?csv=1 gives CSV and ?hours=N limits the window.

Energy Stats

Estimates consumption (kWh) from load over time — 24-hour and log-window totals, average power and optional cost if energy_rate is set in nmc.conf.

Storage & Retention

  • Logs live in /var/lib/apc-nmc/events.json and datalog.json.
  • [datalog] interval (sampling gap) and retention limits are configurable in nmc.conf.
  • When troubleshooting, check the Event Log first — commands, logins and delivery failures all land there.
